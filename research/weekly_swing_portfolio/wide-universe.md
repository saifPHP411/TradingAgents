# Twenty-four USDT perpetual contracts

The Bybit Demo scanner defaults to 24 markets. The dashboard uses the worker's configured list. Set TRADINGAGENTS_PERPETUAL_DEMO_SYMBOLS or pass --symbols with a comma- or space-separated list to extend or replace it. The worker validates contract eligibility at start and reports the count scanned each cycle. Market reads are limited to six concurrent tasks. Order handling still runs in one worker, with a 2x leverage ceiling and a two-position cap. Managed positions are processed first.

The 24 contracts in wide-bybit-filters.json were validated against Bybit's public instrument catalog. The historical comparison uses Binance USD-M 15-minute trade and mark candles, funding, and both original and expanded lists on matching weeks. For the 19 added symbols it uses current Bybit quantity-step and minimum-notional values as approximations for the historical replay. All five original contracts retain their modeled limits. Research leverage and fills are modeled separately from Demo execution. The strategy configurations and historical periods were previously examined.

At base costs, aggressive flow went from 42 simulated trades on five markets to 124 on 24 markets, with 58 winners and 23 traded symbols across base and stress. The Bitcoin-relative momentum model went from 126 trades to 140, with 63 winners and 22 traded symbols across base and stress. Bitcoin is included as the benchmark for that rule. Median final balances fell to 7.93 and 34.21 USDT, respectively, starting from 50 USDT each week. No run met the combined 1,000 USDT and 75% thresholds. See wide-universe-weekly.csv and wide-universe-coverage.json for the results.
